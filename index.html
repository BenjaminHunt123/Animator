Production Duties & Creative Process

The structured, step-by-step pipeline for feature film, TV series, game, and commercial animation.

01

Storyboarding & Animatic Setup

Reviewing director scripts, sketching shot panels, defining camera motion, timing animatics with dialogue/audio tracks, and establishing key beats.

Panels & Audio Track
02

Character Rigging & Topology

Building skeleton deformers, setting up IK/FK limbs, painting mesh skin weights, building custom face shape keys, and testing range-of-motion (ROM).

IK/FK Rigging & ROM
03

Keyframing & Blocking Passes

Posing key story beats on stepped tangents, establishing silhouettes, defining character weight/gravity, and timing key poses against sound cuts.

Stepped Keyframes
04

In-betweening, Splining & Polish

Converting stepped curves to smooth splines, refining arcs, adding overlap/follow-through, micro-editing eye darts, and polishing arcs to perfection.

Spline Polish & Arcs
05

Compositing, Lighting & VFX

Rendering multi-pass layers, setting up rim lights, adding depth-of-field, integrating particle effects, motion blur, and color grading passes.

Render Passes & Grade
06

Director Feedback & Revisions

Submitting dailies/rushes for review, incorporating lead animator notes, tweaking performance timing, and exporting master deliverables.

Dailies & Deliverables
